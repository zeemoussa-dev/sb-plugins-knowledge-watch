1. **Check your interests.** Open [Industry Watch](app:/industry-watch) and choose **Interests**. Six categories ship with topics in each. Rename them, add topics, remove what you do not care about, and choose **Save interests**.
2. **Switch the Pipelines on.** On the [Settings page](app:/settings/plugins/industry-watch), choose **Switch on** for **Industry scan** and for **Industry weekly**. Their schedules read in Dubai time (`CRON_TZ=Asia/Dubai`).
3. **Choose where the digest goes.** Under **Where the digest goes**, pick the person whose chat receives it. Tick **Also email it**, choose the mailbox it is sent from, and choose who receives it, then **Save where it goes**.
4. **See a first digest now** rather than waiting for Sunday. Choose **Run now** on **Industry scan**. When its run has finished (Agent Activity shows it), choose **Run now** on **Industry weekly**.

The first scan looks back seven days. Each later scan looks back to the one before it.
