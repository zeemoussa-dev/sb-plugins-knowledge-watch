## Interests

A **knowledge category** is one beat: "Hyperscalers and cloud", "Sovereign AI and policy". Each one has:

- a **name**, which is also the name of its section in the digest;
- an optional line on **why it matters to you**, which the researcher is given;
- its **topics**: the things to follow inside it, such as "Oracle OCI" or "US export controls on AI chips". Each topic may carry a note on **what you want to know**, such as "licences for the UAE in particular". The researcher searches topic by topic and reads your note.

Switch a category **off** to stop scanning it while keeping its topics. **Put back the shipped ones** replaces your categories with the six the plugin ships, after asking you.

There can be up to 20 categories, with up to 30 topics in each. A change counts from the next scan.

## News

Every item the researcher filed, newest first, kept for 180 days. Each item shows:

- its **importance** (high, medium or low), as the researcher judged it;
- the **title**, linking to the source;
- the **source**, its **date**, and the category and topic it was filed under;
- a **summary** of what happened, as reported;
- **why it matters**: what it means for Core42. This is the researcher's inference, never reported fact;
- the **organisations** it names.

Filter by category, period or words.

A story is filed once: the same link found again is refused, with tracking parameters ignored. A source on the blocked list is never filed.
